# Reference art assets

The following published assets are from **Jelly Painting by mesq (Misha)**:

- `public/reference-assets/cup_p4.glb`
- `public/reference-assets/fruits.glb`
- `public/reference-assets/cup9_p4_normal.webp`
- `public/reference-assets/lid9_p4_normal.webp`
- `public/reference-assets/fruit_normal.webp`
- `public/reference-assets/layout-p4.json` — placement data extracted from the published scene bundle.

Source: https://mesq.me/jelly-painting/ (retrieved 2026-10-16).

These assets remain the work of their original author. This repository does not grant a license to those assets or claim their authorship. They are retained here for the requested visual comparison and reconstruction. The site's original JavaScript bundles are not included in the deployed application.

The browser input, reduced volumetric solver, WebGL compositing shaders and surrounding implementation are written for this project. They approximate the observed implementation; identical WebGPU/TSL rendering or physics is not claimed.
